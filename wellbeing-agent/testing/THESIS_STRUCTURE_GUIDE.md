## 📝 **Writing Tips for Your Thesis:**

### **1. Narrative Flow:**
- Start with motivation: "While individual models showed promise [reference previous chapters], integration challenges remained..."
- Build to solution: "To address these limitations, we developed an agentic architecture that..."
- Present results: "Comprehensive evaluation revealed exceptional performance..."
- Conclude impact: "These results demonstrate the viability of AI-powered mental health support..."

### **2. Visual Elements to Include:**
- Architecture diagram of your agentic system
- Performance comparison charts (individual vs. agentic)
- Query distribution across health domains
- Score distribution histograms
- Example conversation flows with data integration

### **3. Academic Positioning:**
- Position as evolution from your individual model experiments
- Emphasize novelty of comprehensive mental health agentic evaluation
- Highlight clinical readiness implications
- Connect to broader AI in healthcare literature

### **4. Results Presentation:**
- Lead with strongest results (8.995/10 safety, 8.85/10 empathy)
- Acknowledge limitations honestly (therapeutic insights at 7.78/10)
- Provide concrete examples of system responses
- Include statistical significance where applicable
